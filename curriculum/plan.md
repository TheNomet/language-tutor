## The progression

Grouped into CEFR-aligned **stages**, each containing **units** (roughly one week each).
Every unit states its **communication goal**, the **grammar** that serves it, and the
**situations** to practice. Suggested vocabulary is listed inline as a starting point.

Milestone markers (⭐) mark levels worth celebrating and, where relevant, where a real
exam would test you.

### STAGE 0 — Reboot & foundations (pre-A1)

- **Unit 0.1 — Sounds & survival.** Alphabet/script, tricky sounds, greetings, "my name
  is…", "I'm from…", numbers 0–20. *Situation: introduce yourself.*
- **Unit 0.2 — To be / to have + basic sentence structure.** The copula, "have", and the
  language's default word order. Simple statements & questions.
  *Situation: describe your family.*
- **Unit 0.3 — Present tense & everyday verbs.** Regular present, common verbs, negation.
  *Situation: your daily routine.*

⭐ **Checkpoint A1-in-sight:** introduce yourself, your family, and your normal day, out
loud, without notes.

---

### STAGE 1 — A1: Everyday basics

- **Unit 1.1 — Nouns, articles & gender.** The gender/article system; indefinite vs
  definite. *Situation: what's in your bag / your home.*
- **Unit 1.2 — Plurals.** Regular plurals + common irregulars.
  *Situation: shopping list, "how many…".*
- **Unit 1.3 — Pronouns, subject & object.** Personal pronouns in their forms.
  *Situation: talking about other people.*
- **Unit 1.4 — Possessives.** Possessive forms (and reflexive possessives if the
  language has them). *Situation: whose things are whose.*
- **Unit 1.5 — Questions.** Question words; yes/no question formation.
  *Situation: asking for information.*
- **Unit 1.6 — Numbers, time, dates, prices.** Clock, days, months, money.
  *Situation: appointments, opening hours, paying.*
- **Unit 1.7 — <The learner's #1 real use case>.** Vocabulary + set phrases for the
  situation they most need. *Situation: handle that situation unaided.*

⭐ **Milestone — A1 reached.** Short, simple exchanges about predictable everyday needs.

---

### STAGE 2 — A2: Coping with everyday situations

- **Unit 2.1 — Past tense.** *Situation: what happened yesterday.*
- **Unit 2.2 — Perfect / compound past (if the language marks it).**
  *Situation: "I have already …".*
- **Unit 2.3 — Modal / auxiliary verbs.** *Situation: requests & intentions.*
- **Unit 2.4 — Word order deeper: subordinate clauses.** Clause connectors & any word-
  order shifts they trigger. *Situation: reasons & conditions.*
- **Unit 2.5 — Adjectives & agreement.** Agreement + comparison (comparative/superlative).
  *Situation: describing people/things.*
- **Unit 2.6 — Health & doctor.** Symptoms, body parts, booking an appointment.
  *Situation: call the doctor / report illness.*
- **Unit 2.7 — Shopping, services, getting around.** Shops, quantities, directions,
  transport. *Situation: actually use the language in public instead of switching.*
- **Unit 2.8 — Small talk & social glue.** Weather, weekend, feelings, polite formulas.
  *Situation: chat with a neighbor.*

⭐ **Milestone — A2 reached.** Do a mock oral if an exam is the goal.

---

### STAGE 3 — B1: Independent everyday user

- **Unit 3.1 — Future & conditionals.**
- **Unit 3.2 — Connected speech.** Linking words; telling a longer story in sequence.
- **Unit 3.3 — Opinions & discussion.** Agreeing/disagreeing, giving reasons, hedging.
- **Unit 3.4 — Reported speech & indirect questions.**
- **Unit 3.5 — Passive & impersonal constructions.** *Reading real notices and letters.*
- **Unit 3.6 — Work & bureaucracy.** Appointments, forms, emails, official phone calls.
- **Unit 3.7 — Society & culture prep.** If the goal includes a citizenship/society test,
  learn the relevant civics *in the target language* (doubles as reading practice).
- **Unit 3.8 — Fluency & polish.** Pronunciation, error clean-up, spontaneous
  conversation on varied topics.

⭐ **Milestone — B1 reached.** Do a full mock (oral + written) if an exam is the goal.

---

## After B1

Maintain and stretch: read real material (simplified then normal), watch native media
with subtitles, keep speaking regularly.
